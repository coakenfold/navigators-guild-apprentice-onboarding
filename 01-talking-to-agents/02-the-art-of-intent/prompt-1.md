## What I'm Building

A personal recipe collection web app where I can save, search, and organize my own recipes in one place instead of scattered across bookmarks and notes.

## Who It's For

Just me. It's a single-user tool for my own cooking.

## What It Should Do

- Add recipes manually through a simple form (title, ingredients, steps)
- Tag or categorize each recipe (e.g. dinner, vegetarian, dessert)
- Search recipes by keyword
- Filter recipes by tag or category
- View a recipe clearly while cooking
- Edit and delete recipes

## What It Should Look Like

Bright and playful: colorful, friendly, and lively. It should be mobile-first, with large readable text and easy tap targets since I'll mostly use it on my phone in the kitchen.

## What It Should NOT Do

- No accounts or login
- No social features (sharing, comments, public profiles)
- No ads or paywalls
- No AI-generated recipes; only recipes I add myself

## Additional Context

- Runs as a web app in the browser
- Data is stored locally on the device (local storage), with no backend or database
- Phone is the primary device, so it should work well on small screens
