## What I'm Building

A reading list tracker web app where I can add books, mark them as read, and rate them, so I have one place to keep track of what I want to read and what I've finished.

## Who It's For

Just me. It's a single-user tool for my own reading.

## What It Should Do

- Add books manually (title and author)
- Organize books into status shelves: want to read, reading, and read
- Mark a book as read
- Rate books from 1 to 5 stars
- Search books by title or author
- Filter books by status or rating
- Show reading stats, such as books read per year and average rating
- Edit and delete books

## What It Should Look Like

Warm and cozy, like a small personal library: earthy tones, serif typography, and a comfortable, inviting feel. It should be mobile-first, with easy tap targets and readable text on a phone.

## What It Should NOT Do

- No accounts or login
- No social features (sharing, following, public profiles)
- No book recommendations; only books I add myself
- No ads or paywalls

## Additional Context

- Runs as a web app in the browser
- Data is stored locally on the device (local storage), with no backend or database
- Phone is the primary device, so it should work well on small screens
