```
A personal recipe collection web app where I can save, search, and organize my own recipes in one place instead of scattered across bookmarks and notes.
```

- **Project context:** What you're building and why
- **Technical context:** What technology you're using, what already exists
- **Constraint context:** What the boundaries are
- **Preference context:** How you want it to look and feel

## Project Context

- **What:** A recipe collection web app for saving, searching, and organizing my own recipes in one place.
- **Why:** My recipes are scattered across bookmarks and notes.
- **Who it's for:** Just me. It's a single-user tool for my own cooking.
- **Core features:**
  - Add recipes manually through a simple form (title, ingredients, steps)
  - Tag or categorize each recipe (e.g. dinner, vegetarian, dessert)
  - Search recipes by keyword
  - Filter recipes by tag or category
  - View a recipe clearly while cooking
  - Edit and delete recipes

## Technical Context

- **Platform:** A web app that runs in the browser.
- **Primary device:** My phone, so it needs to work well on small screens.
- **Storage:** Data is stored locally on the device (local storage), with no backend or database.
- **What already exists:** Nothing yet. This is a new project.

## Constraints Context

- No accounts or login
- No social features (sharing, comments, public profiles)
- No ads or paywalls
- No AI-generated recipes; only recipes I add myself
- No backend or database

## Preferences Context

- **Look and feel:** Bright and playful: colorful, friendly, and lively.
- **Layout:** Mobile-first.
- **Readability:** Large, readable text.
- **Interaction:** Easy tap targets, since I'll mostly use it on my phone in the kitchen.
